# Literature Review Outline

Teacher Professional Knowledge
        ↓
Teaching Practice
        ↓
Student Achievement

Also note that the empirical relationships may not necessarily form a complete causal mediation chain.

## 1. Teacher Knowledge as a Foundation for Teaching Quality

Purpose:
Define teacher professional knowledge and explain why content knowledge and pedagogical knowledge may matter for instruction.

## 2. From Teacher Knowledge to Classroom Practice

Purpose:
Review whether stronger teacher knowledge translates into improved instructional practice.

## 3. From Classroom Practice to Student Learning

Purpose:
Review evidence linking teaching practice, including active/learner-centered instruction, with student achievement.

## 4. Connecting Teacher Knowledge and Student Achievement

Purpose:
Review direct and indirect evidence linking teacher professional knowledge with student outcomes.

## 5. Evidence and Research Gaps in LMIC Contexts

Purpose:
Explain what is known in developing-country settings and identify the specific contribution of my study.
