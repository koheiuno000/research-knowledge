## 2. Research Question

What does **existing empirical evidence** indicate about the **short- and long-run effects of natural disasters on human capital**, and how robust and comparable are those findings across contexts and outcomes?

## 4. Conceptual Focus

- Organizes **disaster → human capital** evidence before many later climate-education syntheses (including Venegas et al. 2024).
- Treats **education** as part of **human capital** alongside health and labor-market outcomes.
- Emphasizes **heterogeneity**, **timing of exposure**, and **data limitations** across disaster contexts.

## 5. Evidence base and review approach
