Standard errors clustered at **barangay** (Table 3).

**Core contribution:** **Quasi-experimental** evidence that **early-childhood typhoon housing damage** widens **grade-completion gaps** through **2005** and lowers **IQ** in **1991–1994**, while **weight/height-for-age** effects are **null** in main Table 3.

## 2. Research Question

Do **early-childhood typhoon housing damages** cause persistent gaps in **education progression**, **cognitive test scores**, and **health** through adolescence and early adulthood?

## 13. Key Takeaway

**Early typhoon housing damage** predicts **persistent schooling gaps** that **widen** with age and **short-run IQ losses**; **physical health** outcomes largely **null** in Table 3.
