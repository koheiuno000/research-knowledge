# Climate Change & Education

Research area for **extreme weather**, **climate shocks**, and **education outcomes** (learning, schooling, disruption, adaptation).

Structure matches the repository standard:

- `academic-papers/` — one summary per reviewed paper (primary category folder `01_`–`07_`)
- `literature-review/` — bibliography, taxonomy, synthesis, generated `literature_review.md` / `.html`
- `academic-papers/pdf/` — local full texts (Git-ignored)

**Evidence base (reviewed):** 6 papers — 2 reviews/synthesis (Venegas et al. 2024; Baez et al. 2010) and 4 primary folders `01`–`06` summaries (3 quasi-experimental, 1 descriptive).

Build: `python scripts/build_literature_review.py --area climate-education` (also refreshes root dashboard).
