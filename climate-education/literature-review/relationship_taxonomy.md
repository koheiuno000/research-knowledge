# Climate Change & Education — Provisional relationship taxonomy

Machine-readable list: `relationship_taxonomy.json`.

Grounded in **Venegas Marin, Schwarz, and Sabarwal (2024)**, Figure 1 (*Impacts of Climate Change on Education Outcomes*), with labels adapted to this repository’s Evidence Mapping conventions.

## Core and distal pathways (Evidence Map)

1. **Weather Shock → Learning** — Short-run learning outcomes (tests, learning progress). **Outcomes** name the specific measure in Effect Summary rows from **primary** studies only.

2. **Weather Shock → Schooling** — Enrollment, dropout, attendance, progression, attainment.

3. **Weather Shock → School Disruption** — Access, closures, infrastructure damage, school environment (including heat exposure at school).

4. **Weather Shock → Household Vulnerability** — Distal channel: income shocks, coping, reduced demand for schooling.

5. **Weather Shock → Health & Nutrition** — Distal channel: readiness and capacity to learn via health/nutrition.

6. **School Disruption → Learning** — Instructional interruption → learning loss. Mark **mediation** only when a **primary study** identifies that pathway; reviews may note synthesized patterns without mediational identification.

7. **Adaptation → Educational Outcomes** — **Intervention/resilience** evidence (programs, infrastructure, policy reforms with educational outcomes). **Not** satisfied by conceptual adaptation discussion alone.

## Evidence typing rules

| Situation | How to record |
|-----------|----------------|
| Narrative review summarizes primary literature | Evidence Mapping: **Review synthesis**; **no** Effect Summary rows copying cited coefficients |
| Original impact paper | Evidence Mapping + Effect Summary as usual |
| Conceptual arrow in Figure 1 without cited causal evidence | **No** or **Conceptual** in Evidence Mapping |
| Total weather effect vs closure-mediated effect | Use **Notes**; do not conflate total and mediation without primary-study design |

## Inclusion / exclusion

- **Include** relationships that match Figure 1’s direct (proximate) and indirect (distal) logic.
- **Exclude** treating this review as an original weather-shock impact evaluation.
- **Exclude** duplicating effect sizes from Table 1 / cited studies into this review’s Effect Summary.
