Impacts of Extreme Weather Events on Education Outcomes: A Review of Evidence

DOI

The World Bank Research Observer, vol. 39, no. 2 (2024), pp. 177–226.

Venegas Marin, Schwarz & Sabarwal (2024) · Narrative / selective review of evidence · Multi-country (synthesis) · Narrative / selective review of e…

Publication

The World Bank Research Observer, vol. 39, no. 2 (2024), pp. 177–226.

Narrative / selective review of evidence

Research Question

What does available evidence show about how extreme weather events affect education outcomes, through direct channels (access, closures, infrastructure, school environment) and indirect channels (household economic shocks, health, nutrition, conflict, displacement), and what are implications for adaptation and evidence gaps?

What the Paper Does Not Establish

Original causal effect sizes; full systematic coverage; verified Adaptation → Educational Outcomes effects; that candidate papers have been independently reviewed in this repository.

Main Findings (summary)

  • World Bank narrative review and Figure 1 framework mapping direct/proximate and indirect/distal pathways from extreme weather to learning, schooling, and long-run outcomes, with hazard-specific synthesis and policy implications.
