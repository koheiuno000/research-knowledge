Publication

Narrative review

School Hazard Vulnerability and Student Learning

DOI

International Journal of Educational Research, vol. 91, pp. 117–131 (2018).

David et al. (2018) · Descriptive / correlational · Philippines · Descriptive / correlational

Publication

International Journal of Educational Research, vol. 91, pp. 117–131 (2018).

Descriptive / correlational

Research Question

How does school hazard vulnerability (exposure to natural and man-made hazards and secondary disruptions such as evacuation center use) relate to patterns in student learning gains/losses on national achievement tests across provinces and hazard types?

Main Findings (summary)

  • Descriptive/correlational mapping of school hazard vulnerability and achievement gain/loss patterns across Philippines provinces—shows heterogeneous associations by hazard type, not causal weather impacts. Verified against local PDF: academic-papers/pdf/David-2018-ijer.pdf. ---

Impacts of Extreme Weather Events on Education Outcomes: A Review of Evidence

DOI

The World Bank Research Observer, vol. 39, no. 2 (2024), pp. 177–226.

Venegas Marin, Schwarz & Sabarwal (2024) · Narrative / selective review of evidence · Multi-country (synthesis) · Narrative / selective review of e…

Publication

The World Bank Research Observer, vol. 39, no. 2 (2024), pp. 177–226.

Narrative / selective review of evidence

Research Question

What does available evidence show about how extreme weather events affect education outcomes, through direct channels (access, closures, infrastructure, school environment) and indirect channels (household economic shocks, health, nutrition, conflict, displacement), and what are implications for adaptation and evidence gaps?

What the Paper Does Not Establish

Original causal effect sizes; full systematic coverage; verified Adaptation → Educational Outcomes effects; that candidate papers have been independently reviewed in this repository.

Main Findings (summary)

  • World Bank narrative review and Figure 1 framework mapping direct/proximate and indirect/distal pathways from extreme weather to learning, schooling, and long-run outcomes, with hazard-specific synthesis and policy implications (publication year 2024; DOI 10.1093/wbro/lkae001). All bibliographic information verified against the local PDF (OUP/WBR, DOI on p. 1). Local filename “Marin et al. (2025, WB).pdf” does not change bibliographic 2024 year. ---
